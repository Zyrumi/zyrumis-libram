# CurseForge project text

## Summary (the one line under the name)

Paladin helper for Forever: blessing and aura reminders with one-key buffing, a Tank / DPS switch and a class quest checklist. No setup.

## Description (paste into the description editor, Markdown)

**Zyrumi's Libram** is a paladin helper made for World of Warcraft: Forever. It works the moment you log in. There is no assignment grid to fill in and nothing to set up, and it stays out of sight until something actually needs you.

### Buffing in one key

When your aura, your own blessing or a group member's blessing is missing or about to run out, an icon appears above your character. Click it to cast, or bind **Cast the next buff** and keep pressing it until the icons are gone: buffing a whole group becomes one key.

Each person gets the blessing that suits their class and role. Warriors and rogues get Might, casters and healers get Wisdom, and tanks and hunters get Kings, because Might only adds melee attack power on Forever. If another paladin already gave someone that blessing, they get their next best one instead, and your own blessing on someone is never swapped for another. You can right-click an icon to choose a different blessing for that person, or set one per class in the settings.

In raids it uses Greater Blessings when you carry Symbols of Kings, and it can restock those for you at vendors, never more than you can pay for. It also reminds you about Righteous Fury, but only while you are tanking. The icons hide during fights, while you are mounted or on a flight path, and if the game won't say what someone has, it shows nothing rather than guessing.

### Tank and DPS in one click

Two buttons sit above your action bar. **Tank** puts on your tank gear set, turns your action bar to your tank page and casts Righteous Fury; **DPS** does the opposite. The Tank button toggles, so a single key switches you back and forth. The bar swap works even in combat, and your gear catches up once the fight is over.

### Every class quest, in one place

Click the minimap button to see every paladin class quest chain on Forever for your faction and race, including the new Forsaken paladin chains: Redemption, the level 20 weapons, the armour and weapon quests along the way, and both mounts up to the Charger. Each chain shows its next step, who gives it and where they stand. Steps tick themselves off as you finish them, and a line in chat tells you when a new class quest opens up at your level.

### For tanks and groups

Threat icons on enemy nameplates show whether a mob is yours, slipping, or on someone else, and name who has it. When a fight ends while you are tanking, a warning pops up if a healer is below 20% mana, so you don't pull too early. Small cleanse buttons beside your own and each party member's frame let you cleanse anyone with one click.

### Settings

Everything can be switched off, moved and resized. Right-click the minimap button for the settings, or find them under Esc > Options > AddOns > Zyrumi's Libram. A few chat commands:

- `/libram quests` opens the class quest checklist
- `/libram move` unlocks the frames so you can drag them
- `/libram status` shows what the buff reminders see right now
- `/libram help` lists the rest

### Good to know

Libram is built around Forever's own spells and quests, not Classic's. Spell names come from the game, so it works in any client language, though its own text is English. In raids where PallyPower is loaded, it leaves group blessings to PallyPower.
