# CurseForge project text (paste into the project description)

**Summary (one line):**
Paladin helper for Forever: blessing and aura reminders with one-key buffing, a Tank / DPS switch, seal timers, threat and more. No setup.

**Description:**

Zyrumi's Libram is a paladin helper that works the moment you log in. No assignment grid, no setup, and nothing on screen until something needs you.

**Buff reminders with one-key buffing**
- An icon appears when your aura, your own blessing or a group member's blessing is missing or running low. Click it to cast.
- Bind "Cast the next buff" and press it until the icons are gone.
- Every group member gets the blessing that suits their class and role. Hunters never get Might (melee only on Forever).
- Someone already blessed by another paladin gets their next best.
- Right-click to choose for one person, or set it per class.
- Greater blessings when you carry Symbols of Kings, plus optional restocking at vendors.
- Righteous Fury reminder, only while you tank.

**Tank / DPS buttons**
One click swaps your gear set, your action bar page and Righteous Fury. The Tank button toggles, so a single key switches back and forth.

**Seal and judgement timers**
Your seal and its time left, "No seal" when you forgot, and your judgement on the target.

**Threat for tanks**
An icon on each enemy nameplate: yours, slipping, or on someone else, with their name.

**Healer mana after combat**
Tanking? A warning when a healer is under 20% mana, so you don't pull too early.

**Cleanse buttons**
A small button beside each party frame. One click cleanses that person.

**Class quest checklist**
Every paladin class quest chain on Forever for your faction and race, including the new Forsaken paladin chains and the Charger. See what is done, what is next and exactly who gives it and where. It ticks itself off, and tells you in chat when a new one opens up.

Everything can be moved, resized, switched off and changed in Options > AddOns > Zyrumi's Libram, from the minimap button or with /libram.

Works with any client language for spell names. Raid blessings are left to PallyPower when it is loaded.
